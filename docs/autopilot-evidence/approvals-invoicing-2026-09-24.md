## Wiring into `agents.status` / `agents.resume` (done after W13 shipped 55aa7bb; test: agent-control.test.ts "supervision and bounded recovery through app control")

A whole-file ship would have committed their work under my message, so the wiring waits until W13 ships. Applied to them on 2026-09-24 it passed `agent-control.test.ts` + `agent-control-server.test.ts` (100/100) and `tsc`; the files were then restored untouched. Run it with `node artifacts/w12/wire-agent-control.cjs` after W13's ship, add an `agents.status`/`agents.resume`/`agents.supersede` test, and ship those files. What it does:

- **Imports** of `agent-supervision`, `coworker-recovery` and `ApprovalReviews`.
