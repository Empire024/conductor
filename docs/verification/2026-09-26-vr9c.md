## Findings (INFO, not reopening)

- **The stub cannot exercise the "server still processing" branch of the stall rule.** With a test endpoint,
  `wiring.ts` answers the watchdog's health probe with `{healthy:true}` and no `processing` flag, so in stub mode
  every silent model call counts as "not processing". The stub proves the 180 s window and the block. It cannot
  prove that a llama-server busy evaluating a long prompt is left alone. That branch is covered only by
  `watchdog.test.ts` and by the real model at night.
- **D1's loop wait is loose.** The committed `--loop-case` asserts only `status === 'blocked'` within 20 min. D2
  adds the reason, the replan count and order, a time bound, and the neighbour that must not block.
