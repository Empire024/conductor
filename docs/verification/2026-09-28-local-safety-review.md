What holds:

- **Nothing new can kill.** `reprobeUnreadable` only lists processes. `safeClose` only moves entries from `unresolved` to `notes`. No terminate call was added.
- **A still-present pid is never marked clean.**
  - A pid still listed stays pending, whether it is readable or not (`row.pid === pid`). After 10 s it stays unresolved.
  - A failed inventory stops the probe and adds its own unresolved entry. Every pending entry also stays unresolved.
