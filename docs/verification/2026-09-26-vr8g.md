### 1fca9e10 loading cursor (regression)
- **C1** (VR8d smoke, unchanged, 2/2):
  - K1, the owner's words "things load and idk if i clicked": a real Refresh on a ~2.8k-file clone shows `progress` and
    aria-busy at 116 ms and clears 53 / 31 ms after the work ends.
  - K2: a fast click shows nothing.
  - K3, a 400 ms poller around the click: tail 34 / 4 ms after the click's own work, 2 transitions, 0 after 3 s. On the
    pre-fix build VR8d measured a ~2.0 s tail and 12 transitions.
- **C2 (new K4, 2/2)**: the click handler awaits call A, then its continuation starts B, then a render it posted (a
  MessageChannel task) starts C. Each call takes ~480 ms in main, and the 400 ms poller runs throughout.
  - The cursor is on at 118 / 128 ms and off 1 ms after C ends. There are 0 transitions after 3 s, and the poller does
    not extend it. The posted-task call C counts as the click's own work.
  - Control: the poller alone for 5 s moves nothing, 2/2. On 3a66883, K4 FAIL with a 542 ms tail.
  - **Finding (report only):** between chained calls the cursor goes **off for 107–118 ms** and then on again (2 gaps
    per run, 2/2). Cause: `BusyWork.settle()` hides a shown call at once when its `minShowMs` is spent, and the
    continuation's next call only shows after `showAfterMs` (100 ms). So a click whose work is a chain of slow calls
    blinks the cursor once per link. Test for the next batch: in `busy-work.test.ts`, a gesture whose first call settles
    after 400 ms and whose continuation starts a second 400 ms call must keep `setAppBusy` true across the settle (no
    false→true toggle). Or rerun `smoke-verify-vr8g-cursor.mjs`, where K4 turns PASS when `gapsMidChainMs` is empty.
