## The soak (durable-jobs-verification D6)

The executor started the 6 h soak at 2026-09-25T08:52:02Z with `scripts/smoke-rv1-d6-soak.mjs`. That is the D1 prose
fixture, rewritten to say "create INDEX.md if it does not exist", in a loop at the default fraction.
- Record: `artifacts/verification/2026-09-25-rv1/durable/soak.json`. Log: `…/durable/soak-6h.log`.
- The run cannot last 6 h as launched. `scripts/smoke-lock.mjs` (an uncommitted change in the tree by another agent)
  now kills a run at its hard timeout, 20 min by default, and when its parent is gone.
- Running it under the lock with `--timeout-min 380` would hold the machine-wide smoke lock for 6 h and stall every
  other verifier's smokes.
- **The controller has to decide one of two things:** run the soak under the lock at a quiet time, or give durable-job
  soaks their own GPU-only lane outside the Electron smoke lock.
- The result lands as a follow-up to docs/durable-jobs-acceptance-2026-09-24.md once it has run.

## Failing scenarios for the next batch (write these as tests)

1. **local-models-save-tokens A2.** Call `run_and_summarize({command:'node -e "setTimeout(()=>{},700000)"'})` with no `timeoutSec`. The call returns at about 120 s. At 610 s, no process from that command may be alive, including the whole process tree on Windows. Also give the caller a way to collect the final exit code and summary after the early return, either a job handle or a status call on the log.
2. **local-models-save-tokens S11.** `tools.list` advertises a savings method (or `usage.limits` carries `localSavings`) returning the same figure as the Usage view. Every `local_ask` call appends a ledger line. Re-run V1 S7 and S13b in a fresh instance before any `run_and_summarize` call.
3. **19c298e4, the fix's own smoke.** `node scripts/smoke-lock.mjs -- node scripts/smoke-v3-s12-restart.mjs` fails 3 of 3 on HEAD. In the streaming case, `app.state.pendingQuitConfirmation` does not name the running tab within 5 s after `app.restart{force:false}` has answered `confirmationPending:true` for it. Either the dialog is raised late or not at all, or the smoke's 5 s wait is wrong. Also, the "dialog closes itself once the work stops" behaviour is untested by RV1.
4. **durable-jobs-as-local-option.** Pick Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B (or whichever local model is loaded) in the launcher. The durable toggle must apply to that model. The failure today: a separate "Local model for durable work" picker defaults to Ornith 1.5 9B. Put the toggle on the chosen local-model entry, or at least default the picker to the loaded or last-used local model.
5. **durable-jobs-verification: completion without evidence.** A stage with `completionCriteria: ['LOG.md has 100000 lines']` must not settle `completed` while LOG.md does not exist. Today it settles in 442 s (D5), and in D1 run 1 stage 1 "completed" with 0 of 150 lines. Check the criteria that can be checked (file exists, line count), or have a fresh-context verifier check them, before completing.
6. **durable-jobs-verification: the default-fraction rollover.** 150 prose files × 2 KB, "one-sentence summary per file into INDEX.md, in order", default `contextRolloverFraction`. The job completes with at least 3 in-stage rollovers, each carrying `attemptCredited:true` in `jobs.events`, and INDEX.md has 150 ordered lines. It must also not stop on "The turn was stopped" three times, or on the loop guard for reading a file the stage is supposed to create.
